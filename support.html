Premium Subscriptions

Vegan or Nah? offers an optional Premium subscription that provides additional features, including a higher daily scan limit and access to Saved Scan History.

  • Premium subscribers receive access to up to 50 completed scans per day.
  • Premium subscribers can access Saved Scan History while their subscription is active.
  • Subscriptions automatically renew unless auto-renewal is turned off through your Apple Account subscription settings.
  • Canceling auto-renewal does not immediately remove Premium access. Premium features remain available until the end of the current paid subscription period.
  • After the subscription expires, the app returns to the Free plan and its applicable daily scan limit.

You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple Account subscription settings.

Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased Premium and the app is not recognizing your active subscription, open the Premium screen and select Restore Purchases.

Restore Purchases checks your Apple purchase history and restores access when an eligible active subscription is associated with your Apple Account.

Restoring purchases does not create a new charge.

Scanning Tips

For the most reliable ingredient scan results:

  • Take or choose a clear, close-up photo of the ingredient list.
  • Make sure the ingredient text is well lit and in focus.
  • Fill as much of the image as possible with the ingredient label.
  • Avoid glare, heavy shadows, motion blur, or partially covered text.
  • Try to keep the ingredient list reasonably straight in the image.
  • If the app cannot read the label, try taking another clearer photo.

Scan Limits

Free and Premium users have different daily completed-scan limits. Your remaining scan allowance is displayed inside the app.

If your daily limit has been reached, the app will display when more scans become available.

Upgrading to Premium increases the daily completed-scan limit. Scans already completed during the current daily usage period still count toward that day's total.

Important Information

Vegan or Nah? is designed to help users review ingredient information, but automated scanning and ingredient analysis may not always be accurate or complete.

Always verify product packaging and ingredient information yourself, especially when making decisions related to allergies, dietary restrictions, medical needs, or other important health considerations.
